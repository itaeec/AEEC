Our Rooms

Small groups and a calm space for every age, from 6 weeks to 6 years.

We believe smaller room sizes encourage closer relationships between children and educators, and help every child get the most from their learning. Our early education rooms cater for 12 children and our kindergarten rooms for 22. For children under 3, we have one educator for every four children. For children aged 3 to 5, we have one educator for every eleven children.

Room 1 — Nursery

Our youngest room, for children aged 6 weeks to 18 months. It has a capacity of 12 children.

Room 1 has two sleep rooms to help even the fussiest sleeper settle, and its own outdoor yard with a big sandpit, plenty of room for those exciting first steps, and a large undercover area for shelter from rain or sun.

Room 2 — Toddler

Our toddler room is for children aged 18 months to 2.5 years, with a capacity of 12 children.

There’s a big room for all the learning that’s happening, and a lovely yard with a corner sandpit and a wooden stage for dancing, singing and imaginary play. The yard is partly undercover, so outdoor play happens rain or shine.

Room 3 — Kinder Readiness

Our younger kindergarten room is for children aged 2.5 to 3.5 years. It caters for 22 children, led by a full-time kindergarten teacher and a team of diploma-trained co-educators.

Room 3 offers a funded 3-year-old kinder program, integrated into our long day care program, so children from the age of three all take part in kindergarten.

Room 4 — Kindergarten

The focus of this room is school readiness, for children aged 3.5 to 6 years, with a capacity of 22 children. It’s led by a full-time kindergarten teacher and a team of diploma-qualified educators.

Room 4 runs a funded 4-year-old kindergarten program, integrated into long day care. Read more about our kindergarten program or browse the photo gallery.
